How to Submit a Request
7 simple steps
1
Open the Portal
Go to https://cio-bago.digital on any browser — Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.
2
Fill in the Request Form
Enter your Requesting Office, Contact Person, phone number, event details, date, time, and venue.
3
Select the Services Needed
Check all services required. You can select multiple services in a single request.
4
Attach Supporting Files (Optional)
Upload files to Google Drive and paste the link. Set sharing to "Anyone with the link."
5
Accept the Data Privacy Notice
Click the privacy link, scroll to the bottom, then click "I Accept & Understand."
6
Submit and Note Your Control Number
Click "Submit Service Request." Screenshot your Control Number — you'll need it to track your request.
Install on Your Device
No app store needed
1
Open in Chrome
Open https://cio-bago.digital in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
2
Tap the Three-Dot Menu
Tap the three dots in the top-right corner of Chrome.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Select "Add to Home screen" then tap Add. The CIO Portal icon appears on your home screen.
1
Open in Safari
Open https://cio-bago.digital in Safari. This does not work in Chrome on iPhone.
2
Tap the Share Button
Tap the Share icon — the box with an arrow pointing upward — at the bottom of the screen.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen," then tap Add.
1
Open in Chrome or Edge
Visit https://cio-bago.digital in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
2
Click the Install Icon
Look for the install icon in the address bar (monitor with a download arrow). Click it, then click Install.
3
Done
A shortcut appears on your taskbar and desktop. Opens without a browser window.

RA 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012

Data Privacy Notice & Consent Policy

City Information Office (CIO) — City Government of Bago

1. Introduction

The City Government of Bago — City Information Office (CIO) is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

2. Personal Data Collected

When you submit an official service request through this portal, we collect the following personal and organizational information:

  • Requesting Office / Department Name
  • Full Name of Contact Person
  • Official Phone / Viber Number
  • Social Media / Messenger Profile Details
  • Event Details (Activity Name, Date, Time, Location)
  • Requested Services & Additional Remarks

3. Purpose of Data Processing

Your personal information is collected strictly for official municipal administrative functions, specifically:

  • Evaluating, scheduling, and processing your service request.
  • Contacting you for project coordination, clarification, or site deployment.
  • Logging official event documentations in internal LGU system audit logs.

4. Data Protection & Security Measures

We implement enterprise-grade technical, physical, and administrative security measures to prevent unauthorized access, collection, use, disclosure, or modification of your data:

  • Database records are secured using database-level Row Level Security (RLS) and encrypted connections (HTTPS/SSL).
  • Access to request details is strictly restricted to authorized CIO administrative personnel.

5. Data Retention & Disposal

Your personal data will be retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected or to satisfy statutory legal and municipal record requirements. Upon expiration of the retention period, digital records will be securely archived or anonymized in accordance with national archival standards.

6. Rights of the Data Subject

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to be informed about how your data is processed.
  • The right to reasonable access to your personal data held by the CIO.
  • The right to dispute and request correction of inaccurate data.
  • The right to request the suspension or removal of your personal data from our active processing systems.

7. Contact Information

For inquiries or concerns regarding the processing of your personal data, contact:

  • Office: City Information Office (CIO) — Bago City Hall
  • Address: Bago City, Western Visayas, Philippines
