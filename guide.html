How to Submit a Request

Follow these steps to complete your service request

1

Open the Portal

Go to https://cio-bago.digital on any browser — Chrome, Safari, or Firefox. No app download required to submit a request.

2

Fill in the Request Form

Enter your Requesting Office, Contact Person, and Phone / Viber number. Then provide the event name, date, time, and venue.

3

Select the Services Needed

Check all services you require for the event. Some services have additional fields — fill them in as they appear.

You can select multiple services in a single request.
4

Attach Supporting Files (Optional)

If you have a program of activities or reference documents, upload them to Google Drive and paste the shareable link in the form. Make sure the link is set to "Anyone with the link can view."

5

Read and Accept the Data Privacy Notice

Click the link to open the Data Privacy Statement. Scroll to the bottom to enable the acceptance button, then click "I Accept & Understand."

6

Submit Your Request

Click "Submit Service Request." You will receive a Control Number — keep this for tracking your request status.

Screenshot or note your Control Number after submission.
7

Track Your Request

Use the Track Request section on the homepage. Enter your Control Number to check whether your request is Pending, Approved, In Progress, or Completed.

Install the Portal on Your Device

Add it to your home screen for quick access — no app store needed

1

Open in Chrome

Open https://cio-bago.digital using the Google Chrome browser on your Android phone.

2

Tap the Three-Dot Menu

Tap the three dots ( ⋮ ) in the top-right corner of Chrome.

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Select "Add to Home screen" from the dropdown menu. Confirm by tapping Add.

4

Done

The CIO Portal icon will appear on your home screen. Tap it anytime to open the portal — it works like a native app, even offline for cached pages.

1

Open in Safari

Open https://cio-bago.digital using the Safari browser on your iPhone or iPad. (This will not work in Chrome on iOS.)

2

Tap the Share Button

Tap the Share icon — the box with an arrow pointing upward — at the bottom of the screen (or top-right on iPad).

3

Tap "Add to Home Screen"

Scroll down in the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen." Edit the name if you wish, then tap Add in the top-right corner.

4

Done

The CIO Portal icon will appear on your home screen. It opens in full-screen mode without the browser bar.

1

Open in Chrome or Edge

Visit https://cio-bago.digital in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on your computer.

2

Look for the Install Icon

In the browser address bar, look for a computer screen with a download arrow icon on the right side. Click it.

3

Click "Install"

A prompt will appear. Click Install. The portal will open as a standalone desktop app.

4

Done

A shortcut is added to your taskbar and desktop. Open it anytime without needing to open a browser first.

Note: Installing the portal as an app does not require an app store. It uses Progressive Web App (PWA) technology built into modern browsers. No personal data is stored on your device.

Tracking Your Request Status

Know exactly where your request stands at any time

1

Go to the Homepage

Visit https://cio-bago.digital and scroll down to the Track Your Request section.

2

Enter Your Control Number

Type in the Control Number you received after submitting your request, then click Track.

3

Read Your Status

Your request will show one of these statuses:

Pending — Received, awaiting review.
Contacted — CIO has reached out to you.
Approved — Request has been approved.
Declined — Request was not approved.
Completed — Service has been delivered.
